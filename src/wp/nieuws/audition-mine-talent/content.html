Audition new TALENT production MINE

DansBlokTALENT is a development project for semi-professional and advanced dancers, organized by DansBlok and dance studio Tribes, and takes place in Leiden. The dancers are given the opportunity to gain work experience in a semi-professional production, access professional training, and receive personal coaching. 

AUDITION for MINE

A new DansBlokTALENT production
A dance about ownership, division, and connection

In MINE, a wordless conversation unfolds between many contrasting movement languages. Movements collide, merge, withdraw, and seek closeness once more. The performance explores the question of whether you can truly share something that feels — and is believed to be — yours. In a world where we increasingly find ourselves set against one another, we search for what connects all those different perspectives.

The dancers explore how space can be claimed, shared, or lost, and how shifting group dynamics constantly alter the balance between the individual and the whole. Without words, yet with every fiber of their bodies, they search for ways to connect — with each other and with the audience.

MINE invites you to watch, to feel, and perhaps to recognize something of yourself in the movement of another.
