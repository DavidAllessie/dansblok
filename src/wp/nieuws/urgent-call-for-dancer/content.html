About DansBlok:
DansBlok is an all-female collective based in Leiden. Our work is a reaction to conflicts that the makers experience in our current society. It explores themes like dealing with paradoxical expectations, challenging boundaries and the need for true connection. DansBlok is known for their organic and theatrical movement language, strong expression and partnerwork.
